- **Shi'bud nekhasim without shi'bud ha-guf as the alternative analysis** — supported: R. The section heading itself pairs the two mechanisms exactly as the transcript does.
- **Convergence despite differing mechanism** — supported: "Regardless of how one construes a corporation ... everyone agrees that Halakha validates the limited liability rule."
- **Caveat (a), piercing grounds:** fraud is indeed the chapter's halakhic ground for piercing (R. Bloi: "if the corporate owner uses the corporation to commit fraud," plus an eight-factor halakhic list). Undercapitalization, however, appears in the chapter's *Maryland law* discussion ("whether the corporation was grossly undercapitalized"), not in the halakhic factor list. The transcript's "when halakhah would itself pierce the veil (fraud, undercapitalization)" slightly overreaches on the second item.
- **Caveat (b), attribution:** the transcript calls this "A. Y. Warhaftig-adjacent literature." The series author is A. Yehuda Warburg (as the check request itself notes); the CLI does not expose an author field, but Warhaftig is the wrong name and the hedge "-adjacent" does not rescue it. Also worth knowing: the material sits in the index "Rabbinic Authority II" (the preface confirms "the second in a series"), even though Sefaria's edition line carries the series title and the 2013 date the transcript repeated. The transcript is faithful to the fetched metadata, but a reader hunting the physical 2013 first volume for chapter 7 would land in the wrong book.
