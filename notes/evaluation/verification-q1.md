### 4. Mishnah Bava Metzia 5:1 — definitions of neshekh and tarbit
**VERIFIED.** Torat Emet 357 Hebrew: "אֵיזֶהוּ נֶשֶׁךְ. הַמַּלְוֶה סֶלַע בַּחֲמִשָּׁה דִינָרִין... וְאֵיזֶהוּ תַרְבִּית, הַמַּרְבֶּה בְּפֵרוֹת" — a sela lent for five dinars (neshekh), increase in produce/commodities (tarbit). Davidson English confirms. Editions and licenses match.

### 5. "the sugyot at Bava Metzia 60b–75b" + avaq ribbit
**VERIFIED.** Bava Metzia 60b:8 is the mishnah "Which is neshekh and which is tarbit" (chapter 5, Eizehu Neshekh, begins there); 75b:15 opens the next chapter's mishnah on hiring artisans, so the interest chapter runs 60b–75b as claimed. The term אבק רבית ("dust of interest," avaq ribbit) appears within the range at Bava Metzia 67a:8 ("מִסְתַּבְּרָא כִּי אֲבַק רִבִּית הָווּ"), and 60b:12 itself distinguishes Torah-law from rabbinically prohibited interest, supporting the "rabbis extended the prohibition" framing.

### 6. Mishnah Bava Metzia 5:11 — parties who transgress
**VERIFIED.** Davidson English verbatim: "The lender, and the borrower, and the guarantor, and the witnesses. And the Rabbis say: Also the scribe." Hebrew: הַמַּלְוֶה, וְהַלֹּוֶה, וְהֶעָרֵב, וְהָעֵדִים. וַחֲכָמִים אוֹמְרִים, אַף הַסּוֹפֵר. The "borrower sins too" point is squarely in the text.

### 7. Shulchan Arukh, Yoreh De'ah 160:1 — severity + Rema gloss
**VERIFIED.** Ashlei Ravrevei (Lemberg 1888, public domain, as claimed): "צריך ליזהר בריבית וכמה לאוין נאמרו בו ואפי' הלוה הנותנו והערב והעדים עוברים" — multiple negative commandments, borrower/guarantor/witnesses transgress. The Rema gloss is there, word for word as romanized in the transcript: "הגה ואין חילוק בין אם מלוה לעני או לעשיר" ("there is no distinction whether one lends to a poor person or to a rich person"). The gloss attribution to the Rema at this exact location is correct.

### 8. Shulchan Arukh, Yoreh De'ah 177:2 — the isqa structure
**VERIFIED.** The Hebrew says exactly what the transcript claims: "שכל עיסקא פלגא מלוה ופלגא פקדון" (every isqa is half loan, half deposit); the recipient "טורח בחלק הפקדון בשביל חלק המלוה" (toils on the deposit portion for the sake of the loan portion), "לפיכך צריך לו ליתן שכר טרחו" (therefore the giver must pay him a wage) — i.e., the unpaid-labor-as-disguised-interest rationale; and "ואם פחתו או הותירו יהיה לאמצע בשוה" (loss or profit shared equally), with the Rema requiring the giver to bear full responsibility, including oness, on the deposit half. The transcript's paraphrase is faithful to the Hebrew. (Note: Sefaria's only translation of this passage is an 1898 French one, so the transcript's English rendering had to come from the Hebrew; it checks out.)

### 9. Kitzur Shulchan Arukh 65:28 — gentile intermediary must bear the risk
